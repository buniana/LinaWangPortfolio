About

I’m currently pursuing my Master’s in Human Centered Design & Engineering (HCDE) at the University of Washington, with a background in Chemical Biology and Nutritional Science from UC Berkeley.

Through internships at generative AI and healthcare startups, I’ve discovered my passion for creating designs that make complex technology feel approachable and intuitive.

What Drives Me

I believe the best design feels invisible—where users can accomplish their goals without friction or confusion. I’m drawn to crafting interfaces that feel natural and welcoming, with thoughtful details that make the experience genuinely comfortable.

My Approach

I love getting into the details—iterating on wireframes, refining visual hierarchies, and polishing interactions until everything clicks. Whether I’m building design systems or mapping user flows, I focus on creating solutions that users actually enjoy using.

What I'm looking for
